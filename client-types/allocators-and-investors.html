Software for Security, Transparency, and Control

We aim to provide allocators with enhanced levels of security, transparency, and control around their capital exposure. We have detailed solutions to each of these verticals.

Security: Our DeFi Armor product prevents on-chain capital from being stolen or misappropriated.
Transparency: We provide dashboards with data for security, execution, and AML risks, venue exposure, and more.
Control: We're the first company to enable proper on-chain SMAs. Your assets sit in your segregated account, to which you hold the private keys.

On-Chain SMAs

We support both on-chain and off-chain SMAs. Our off-chain SMA infrastructure is standard with light security enhancements around API Key management. Our on-chain SMA infrastructure is the first of its kind. In our view, we're the first firm to enable proper on-chain SMAs.

You hold the withdrawal keys. Managers hold the trading keys. Trading keys are restricted by a robust DeFi policy engine.
Your capital (your account) is secured by a Safe. You have a separate account for each manager. Your funds sit in a separate smart contract from everyone else's funds.
Allocate to managers directly or subscribe to our Cayman SPC fund, with legally segregated portfolios. FCA regulated AR, BVI IM, Administered by Formidium.
